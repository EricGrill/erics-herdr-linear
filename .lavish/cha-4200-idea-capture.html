What makes it a proper ticket?

  1. A specific title and outcome. Say what changes and why it matters.
  2. Proposed, testable acceptance checks. Distinguish suggestions from facts and completed verification.
  3. Original context. Keep the raw idea and actual evidence links. A local screenshot path is not an uploaded attachment.
  4. Honest unknowns. Ask one useful optional question at a time. Saving the idea stays available.
  5. Safe placement. Correct team/project, conservative defaults, and an explicit execution target when work starts.

Failure states to design first

  • Offline or shaping failed: retain the idea; offer a basic ticket when online.
  • Late shaping result: never replace newer edits.
  • Create response lost: show unconfirmed status and reconcile before retry.
  • Ticket created, launch failed: retry launch against the same Linear ID.
  • Multiple windows: scope drafts by workspace and detect conflicting revisions.

Prove it before shipping

  • Recover drafts across close/reopen and restart.
  • Mock shaping failures, stale replies and missing facts.
  • Create without repository/agent; guard repeated clicks and ambiguous responses.
  • Keep validated launch behavior in both client copies.
  • Run repo checks, touched-mod checks and keyboard/live native inspection.

Capture speed goal: under 15 seconds. This is a target, not a measured result.
